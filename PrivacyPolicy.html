🌟 Privacy overview

Pakistani Drama & Serials is your ultimate destination for the best of Pakistani television — from heart-touching family dramas and intense romance to gripping suspense and socially relevant stories. Enjoy timeless serials and fresh episodes all in one place. We respect your privacy as much as we respect great storytelling. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how you stay in full control — because your privacy deserves a perfect script.

Our commitment: No selling of personal data, minimal permissions, full compliance with COPPA/GDPR-K/CCPA. We're here for the dramas, not for your data.

📊 1. What data we collect

Pakistani Drama & Serials is built to be lightweight and privacy-focused. We only collect anonymous, non‑identifiable information to improve the app and show optional ads:

  • 📱 Device & technical info — model, OS version, language, country, advertising identifier (AAID / IDFA), IP address (anonymized). Used for analytics & ads.
  • 🎮 App preferences & history — favorite dramas, recently watched, saved genres, language preferences. Stored locally on your device.
  • 📢 Ad interaction data — via Google AdMob. AdMob may use identifiers to serve contextual or non-personalized ads. No personal data is shared.
  • 💎 In-app purchases — if you buy premium features or remove ads, Google Play Billing processes the transaction. We never see your payment details.

We never access your contacts, media gallery, location, or any personal files. Pure entertainment.

⚙️ 2. How we use your data

Your information helps us deliver the most engaging and smooth drama experience:

  • Save preferences & watch history — remember your favorite dramas and genres.
  • Analyze & improve performance — using Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics to fix bugs and enhance user experience.
  • Show non-intrusive ads (optional banners and interstitials) to keep the app free.
  • Verify in-app purchases and grant premium features.
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing.

🔌 3. Third‑party services (SDKs)

To deliver core features, we integrate with trusted partners:

  • Google AdMob – advertising. May collect device data and IP for ad relevance. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics – anonymized crash reports and usage metrics. Helps us keep Pakistani Drama & Serials smooth and reliable.
  • Google Play Billing – secure handling of in-app purchases. We never see payment details.

All third parties comply with GDPR, CCPA, COPPA, and other applicable privacy frameworks.

🗑️ 4. Data retention & deletion

Your watch history and preferences remain on your device. Uninstalling Pakistani Drama & Serials removes all local data. No server-side account exists. To request deletion of any analytics data linked to your advertising ID, email us at nadeemgamer180@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

You can reset your advertising ID anytime: Android Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID.

🎯 5. Your ad choices

We respect your advertising preferences:

  • On Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • You may also purchase the “Remove Ads” feature (one‑time IAP) to enjoy a distraction‑free drama experience.
  • For users under the age of consent, only non‑personalized contextual ads are shown.
Child‑safe compliance (COPPA / GDPR‑K) enabled

🧸 6. Children's privacy (COPPA / GDPR‑K)

Pakistani Drama & Serials is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA).

  • No personal data (name, email, contact) is required to browse dramas.
  • If the Play Store identifies a child account, personalized ads are automatically disabled.
  • Parents: if you believe your child has provided any personal information (e.g., via customer support), contact us immediately and we will delete it.

🔒 7. Security measures

We implement industry‑standard security: encrypted network connections (TLS), minimal data retention, and restricted internal access. While no system is 100% invulnerable, we continuously update our practices to protect your information.

📜 8. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location (EEA, UK, California, Brazil, India, Pakistan), you may have the right to:

  • Access any personal data we might hold (linked to advertising ID).
  • Request correction or deletion of such data.
  • Opt out of “sale” of personal data (we never sell).
  • Withdraw consent for analytics by resetting your advertising ID.

To exercise any right, email nadeemgamer180@gmail.com with subject “Privacy Request – Pakistani Drama & Serials”. We will respond promptly.

🔄 9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If changes are significant, we will notify you via an in‑app notice or update the effective date above. Continued use of Pakistani Drama & Serials after changes means you accept the revised policy.
